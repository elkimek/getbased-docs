---
title: "Sync getbased across multiple devices"
description: "Use a 24-word mnemonic to sync your profiles, lab data, and AI settings across devices. The relay stores only encrypted data."
---

getbased can sync your profiles, lab data, and AI settings across as many devices as you like. Sync is opt-in and end-to-end encrypted: a relay server carries the data between your devices, but it only ever stores ciphertext. Without your mnemonic, the relay cannot read anything.

## How it works

Sync is powered by [Evolu](https://www.evolu.dev/), a local-first CRDT (Conflict-free Replicated Data Type) engine. When you enable sync:

1. A **24-word mnemonic** is generated — this is both your sync identity and your encryption key.
2. Your data is encrypted with a key derived from that mnemonic and pushed to the relay.
3. Any other device that uses the same mnemonic can pull and decrypt your data.

The relay acts as a blind store-and-forward server. It holds encrypted blobs and forwards them to your other devices. It never sees your plaintext.

## Set up sync on your first device

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open sync settings">
    Go to **Settings → Data**, or click the **Protect your data** pill on the dashboard and select **Cross-device Sync**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up new sync">
    Click **Set up new sync**. getbased generates a new Sync identity and 24-word mnemonic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your mnemonic">
    Your 24-word mnemonic is displayed in cleartext. Write it down and store it somewhere offline — a piece of paper in a safe place, a password manager, or both. Then check **I have saved my mnemonic somewhere safe**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Click **Done**. Your data begins syncing to the relay immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Anyone who has your mnemonic can access your synced data. Treat it like a password — store it offline and never share it.
</Warning>

## Add more devices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open sync settings on the new device">
    Go to **Settings → Data**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join your existing identity">
    Click **Join existing device**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your mnemonic">
    Paste or type your 24-word mnemonic from your first device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore">
    Click **Restore**. getbased finishes switching the local Sync owner before it performs the controlled page reload, then begins pulling your profiles and settings from the relay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the reload, keep the app open until the header dot turns green. If the device still has no profiles, open **Settings → Data → Cross-device sync**, confirm that its **Sync identity** matches your other device, and choose **Sync now**.

<Warning>
Joining or switching to another Sync identity replaces this browser's local synced profile data. Export a backup first if this browser contains changes that are not present on your other devices.
</Warning>

## Manage a connected device

Open **Settings → Data → Cross-device sync** to manage the current browser:

- **Sync identity** shows a short, safe comparison code. Matching codes mean two devices use the same 24-word identity. The code does not grant access to your data.
- **Restore / switch identity** connects this browser to a different mnemonic and reloads its synced data.
- **Disable on this device** stops sync and clears the Sync identity from this browser. It does not delete relay data or disable your other devices.

To reconnect a disabled browser to the same identity, choose **Join existing device** and restore the saved mnemonic. Choose **Set up new sync** only when you intend to create a separate identity and reconnect your other devices to it.

## What syncs

The following data flows across all paired devices:

- All profile data — lab entries, per-file import history, context cards, notes, supplements, cycle data, custom markers, EMF assessments
- Profile metadata — name, sex, date of birth, location, tags
- AI settings — provider, API keys, model selections, Venice Encrypted TEE Mode toggle, PPQ private-mode preference
- Agent Access state — enabled flag, relay profile metadata, read token, Agent Context key, and the selected wearable daily-series window
- Chat threads, messages, custom personalities, and persona deletion records
- Display preferences — unit system, range mode, chart overlays

Settings such as theme are device-specific and do not sync. Unsent per-conversation chat drafts, wearable OAuth refresh tokens, raw wearable daily rows, and AI model-test history also stay local to each browser even though compact wearable summaries and Agent Access series preferences can sync.

### AI model-test history

Model-test records include timing, loaded-model state, context, and hardware-related details that are meaningful only on the device that ran the test. getbased excludes these records from outbound sync and preserves the receiving device's local history during a merge. Run tests separately on each device if you want to compare their performance.

Confirmed lab results and per-file import history still sync normally. Deleting a model-test record affects only its local diagnostics, not the imported health data. See [Test and compare AI models](/guides/ai-model-testing).

### Wearable data

Wearable sync follows a deliberate split:

- **Wearable summary syncs.** The latest values, baselines, weekly trends, and anomaly events for every connected wearable metric flow to your other devices alongside everything else. Strip cards on Device B show the right numbers immediately after sync.
- **Raw daily rows stay local.** The full 90-day history (every per-day HRV, sleep score, RHR, and manual entry) lives in a per-device IndexedDB and is excluded from the sync payload. Detail-modal charts on Device B will be empty until you connect each wearable vendor separately on that device.
- **OAuth tokens never sync.** Each device must authorise each wearable independently. This is by design — a stolen mnemonic should not grant continuous access to your live wearable feeds.

## Sync status indicator

When sync is enabled, a small colored dot appears in the header next to the settings gear:

| Dot | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green | Synced — relay connected, data confirmed |
| Blue (pulsing) | Syncing — push or pull in progress |
| Amber | Offline — relay unreachable, changes saved locally |
| Red | Error — sync failed |

Click the dot to see relay connectivity, recent push and pull status, relay storage usage, and a **Sync now** button for manual retry. The **Settings** button opens the Data tab directly.

For a clearer health check, open **Settings → Data → Cross-device sync → Advanced → Sync status & storage**. This screen leads with a plain-language result, the identity comparison, relay verification, and storage usage. Technical rows and developer metrics stay collapsed unless you open **Technical details**.

## Relay storage and recovery

Relay storage measures encrypted change history, not just the current size of your health data. Repeatedly saving identical data used to grow that history unnecessarily. getbased now skips equivalent local saves and unchanged sync pushes, so refreshing the app without changing data should not keep consuming relay space.

When the relay supports owner self-service, **Sync status & storage** shows the current usage and the relay's configured limit. Use **Refresh usage** to request an authoritative reading.

If storage reaches the amber or red range:

1. Let every connected device finish syncing.
2. On the device with the most complete data, open **Sync status & storage**.
3. Click **Reduce storage** and keep the tab open.

getbased first sends pending edits from every local profile, pulls the latest relay state, replaces the old encrypted history with fresh snapshots, and verifies the rebuild. Local profile data is not deleted. If compaction succeeds but rebuilding is interrupted, keep the same device online and use **Retry rebuild**.

### Protection when a saved profile cannot be read

Large profile blobs—including imported genome data—can live in IndexedDB even when local encryption is off. Sync reads active and inactive profiles through that canonical storage path.

If a profile is temporarily missing, locked, corrupt, or otherwise unreadable, getbased does not turn the failed read into an empty profile. It skips the outbound update, and **Reduce storage** stops before compaction if any required profile cannot be read. Existing relay data and the other devices' copies remain untouched so you can diagnose the local storage problem without propagating a blank replacement.

## Conflict resolution

Sync uses **last-write-wins per item**, based on timestamps. Supported profile surfaces send small per-item CRDT deltas — one lab entry, one marker note, or one sun session — while compatibility and recovery paths can send a complete encrypted snapshot. If two devices edit the same item before they sync, the most recent update wins. Edits to different items merge independently, and unchanged data is not sent again.

## Mnemonic security

Your mnemonic is your encryption key. getbased takes several precautions to protect it:

- **Masked by default** — displayed as bullet characters in Settings, with a Show/Hide toggle.
- **Clipboard auto-clear** — when you copy the mnemonic, the clipboard is cleared after 60 seconds.
- **No server storage** — the mnemonic is generated and stored locally by the Evolu engine and is never sent to any server.

### Changing your mnemonic

**Disable on this device** affects only the current browser. Relay data and devices already using the old mnemonic remain active. Restoring the old mnemonic reconnects this browser to that identity.

Creating a **New setup** generates a separate identity and mnemonic. To move completely, save the new mnemonic and join that identity from each other device.

<Danger>
If you lose your mnemonic, there is no recovery. You can still access your local data on each device, but you will need to set up sync again from scratch with a new mnemonic on all devices.
</Danger>

## Profile deletion across devices

When you delete a profile, getbased marks it as deleted on the relay (a tombstone). On the next pull, every other paired device sees the tombstone and wipes its local copy automatically — single-profile deletes propagate without prompting.

For batched deletes (two or more profiles deleted at once), the receiving device quarantines the wipe and shows a confirmation UI in **Settings → Data**. Each pending delete has **Apply delete** and **Restore** buttons. This protects against a leaked mnemonic: an attacker publishing tombstones for all your profiles would otherwise silently wipe every paired device on the next pull.

To reject a quarantined delete, click **Restore** — getbased re-publishes the local profile data, which beats the tombstone on the next sync. To accept, click **Apply delete** — the local wipe runs and the entry clears.

## Run your own relay server

Use [getbased-relay](https://github.com/elkimek/getbased-relay), the production wrapper around Evolu's sync protocol. It adds configurable quotas, health and metrics, accurate per-identity storage reporting, and the signed self-service recovery endpoints used by **Sync status & storage**.

The upstream Evolu relay can carry basic encrypted sync traffic, but it does not provide getbased's storage meter or **Reduce storage** workflow. See [Self-host getbased](/guides/self-hosting#running-your-own-sync-relay) for the Docker and reverse-proxy setup.

<Tip>
For a production relay, disable SSH password authentication, enable a firewall (allow only ports 22, 80, and 443), and enable automatic security updates.
</Tip>

## Relationship to local encryption

Cross-device sync and [local encryption](/guides/encryption) are independent systems:

- **Local encryption** (passphrase → AES-256-GCM) protects your localStorage data at rest on each device.
- **Sync encryption** (mnemonic → Evolu's key derivation → XChaCha20-Poly1305) protects data in transit and on the relay.

You can use either, both, or neither. Enabling local encryption does not affect sync, and vice versa.
