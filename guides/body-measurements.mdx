Use **Waist** and **Abdominal depth** in Biometrics to follow manual body measurements over time. You can also add biometric widgets to the dashboard. These records do not require AI or a wearable connection.

## Record your waist

Open the **Waist** card and enter the measurement date, value, and unit: **cm** or **in**.

Choose the method you actually used:

- **Position:** standing, seated, or lying down.
- **Location:** between the lowest rib and hip bone, at the top of the hip bone, or at the navel.
- **Hip:** optional circumference measured using the same recorded method.

The editor includes measurement guidance and remembers your method. Save the reading when it is correct. Use its history controls to edit or delete a saved measurement.

Different positions and locations remain separate in history, including on the same day. For comparisons, repeat the same method rather than treating every waist measurement as interchangeable.

## Read the ratios

- **Waist-to-hip ratio** requires the matching hip measurement.
- **Waist-to-height ratio** requires a valid height in the profile.

Measurements are stored in centimetres and displayed in your selected unit. Changing the display does not rewrite the underlying readings.

## Record abdominal depth

**Abdominal depth**, also called sagittal abdominal diameter or **SAD**, uses a caliper while lying down at the top of the hip bone. It is a different measurement from a tape circumference; enter it in the **Abdominal depth** editor.

## Keep cycle timing consistent

When recorded cycle context is available, measurement history can show the cycle day. For eligible regular natural cycles, getbased can suggest an estimated first-baseline window after bleeding and bloating settle, or a later date comparable with your baseline.

These calendar estimates are a consistency aid, not a validated “best day” for waist or SAD measurements. They do not create observed periods or prove ovulation. Irregular cycles or missing information may produce general guidance instead of dates. Keep your [period log](/guides/menstrual-cycle) up to date and use a similar time of day.

## Sync, export, and chat

Explicit manual readings and their deletion state participate in optional encrypted Sync and supported profile exports. Wearable source data remains separate. Keep an independent [backup](/guides/backup).

You can also ask chat to prepare a supported manual-reading change. Supply the date, value, unit, and method, then review the proposal before applying it. See [Health updates in chat](/guides/health-updates-in-chat).
